Secret gem 4 — Economic calendar rule
Actual vs ForecastReactionAction
Actual ABOVE forecastBullish surprise — market pumps 🟢Confirm with chart — look for 2U entry
Actual BELOW forecastBearish surprise — market dumps 🔴Wait for dust to settle — look for 2D or stand aside
Actual MATCHES forecastMuted reaction — price action takes over 🟡Let Strat setup guide the entry
The master rule

Markets don't react to good or bad numbers.
They react to BETTER OR WORSE THAN EXPECTED numbers.

The Forecast column is the most important number on the calendar — not the Previous.
Previous = the trend. Forecast = what's already baked into price. Actual = does market need to reprice?

Pro move: Never trade INTO the number. Let it drop, let price react 1-2 candles, THEN look for Strat confirmation.

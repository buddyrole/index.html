02

Gamma Regime & Creep

Regime — Where Spot Sits

  • Above flip: supportive, mean-reverting conditions. Weight Put Wall bounces higher.
  • Below flip: momentum-driven conditions. Weight Call Wall rejections higher.

Creep — How Flip Is Migrating

  • Flip shifting UP toward spot → positioning support weakening. Lean bearish after confirmation.
  • Flip shifting DOWN away from spot → positioning turning supportive. Allow more room on confirmed dips.
03

Macro Gravity Check

Macro is the wind, not the trigger. Always ask why yields are moving before applying the bias.

Speedometer — Tactical

  • 5-Yr & 10-Yr both rising → bias puts (tightening)
  • 5-Yr & 10-Yr both falling → bias calls (easing)
  • 5-Yr & 10-Yr disagree → neutral, require stronger confirmation

Dollar Confirmation

  • Yields ↑ + USD strengthening → stronger headwind, favor Call Wall rejections
  • Yields ↓ + USD weakening → stronger tailwind, favor Put Wall bounces
  • Yields & USD disagree → macro incomplete, let price action lead

Weather Forecast — Structural

  • 30-Yr yield above stress threshold (e.g. 5.00%) → long-term valuation warning. Rallies more fragile — reduce size, shorten targets, demand stronger confirmation for calls, prioritize confirmed Call Wall fades.
  • This is a warning, not an automatic bear market and not permission to fade every rally.
04

Overnight Entry Checklist

Put Setup Bearish

  • Price closes within defined distance of Call Wall
  • Tests the wall but fails to hold above it
  • Final 15–30 min shows rejection / weakening momentum
  • Macro & gamma conditions bearish or neutral
  • Enter defined-risk put before close → exit in predetermined opening window
Invalidation: held acceptance above Call Wall

Call Setup Bullish

  • Price closes within defined distance of Put Wall
  • Tests the wall and holds / reclaims it
  • Final 15–30 min shows buyer confirmation
  • Macro & gamma conditions bullish or neutral
  • Enter defined-risk call before close → exit in predetermined opening window
Invalidation: held acceptance below Put Wall
05

Non-Negotiable Execution Rules

  • 01Never trade the first touch without confirmation.
  • 02Define invalidation before entering.
  • 03Risk only a predetermined amount of account equity.
  • 04Do not chase after price has already moved away from the level.
  • 05Check IV, spread width, strike, and expiration before entry.
  • 06Use a time stop if the expected move doesn't begin promptly.
  • 07Do not average down after structural invalidation.
  • 08Major data releases (CPI, FOMC, payrolls, major tech earnings) can override walls and gamma — check the calendar first.
  • 09Recalculate levels after expiration or major OI changes — Barchart official walls update ~8:30pm ET.
  • 10"No trade" is a valid position.
